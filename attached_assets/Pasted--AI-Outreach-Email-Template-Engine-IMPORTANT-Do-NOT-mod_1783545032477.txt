
  
# AI Outreach Email Template Engine
 
**IMPORTANT: Do NOT modify or replace the existing AI email generation system. Extend it by creating a Premium AI Outreach Email Template Engine.**
 
## Objective
 
Build a dynamic email generation engine that creates personalized, human-sounding cold emails using the website analysis already generated by the system.
 
The emails must **never sound like mass marketing or AI-generated content**.
  
## Email Structure
 
### Subject Line
 
Generate 5 subject line variations and select the highest-quality one.
 
Examples:
 
 
- We found a few opportunities on {{BusinessName}}'s website
 
- Quick website review for {{BusinessName}}
 
- A few ideas to improve your website
 
- We analyzed your website
 
- Some opportunities we noticed
 

 
Never use clickbait or spammy words.
  
### Opening
 
Address the business naturally.
 
Example:
 
Hi {{OwnerName or BusinessName Team}},
 
We recently reviewed your website and noticed a few opportunities that could improve customer experience and increase enquiries.
  
### Personalized Observation
 
Mention only 2–3 important findings from the analysis.
 
For example:
 
 
- Missing online booking
 
- No AI chatbot
 
- Weak SEO
 
- No email capture
 
- Slow mobile performance
 
- Missing testimonials
 

 
Do not overwhelm the reader.
  
### Value
 
Explain the business impact.
 
Example:
 
These improvements can help capture more enquiries, improve customer trust, and make it easier for visitors to become paying customers.
 
Avoid unrealistic promises.
  
### Report Section
 
Include automatically:
  
We prepared a free personalized website report for you.
 
View your report here:
 
{{REPORT_URL}}
  
### Soft Call To Action
 
Never sound pushy.
 
Example:
 
If you'd like, I'd be happy to show you how these improvements can be implemented.
 
Simply reply to this email.
  
### Signature
 
Use agency branding automatically.
 
Example:
 
Best regards,
 
{{AgencyName}}
 
{{Website}}
 
{{Email}}
  
## AI Rules
 
Every email must:
 
 
- Be under 180 words.
 
- Read like it was written by a real person.
 
- Avoid sales clichés.
 
- Avoid exaggerated claims.
 
- Avoid generic AI phrases.
 
- Mention only findings that actually exist in the report.
 
- Use natural language.
 
- Maintain a professional tone.
 

  
## Dynamic Personalization
 
The AI should adapt based on the analysis.
 
If the website has poor SEO, discuss SEO.
 
If there is no booking system, discuss bookings.
 
If there is no chatbot, discuss customer support.
 
If mobile performance is poor, discuss mobile usability.
 
Never mention problems that were not detected.
  
## Spam Protection
 
Avoid phrases like:
 
 
- Guaranteed results
 
- Limited time offer
 
- Buy now
 
- Act fast
 
- Amazing opportunity
 
- Earn more instantly
 

 
Keep the email conversational.
  
## A/B Testing
 
Automatically generate three different versions of every email.
 
Label them:
 
 
- Version A
 
- Version B
 
- Version C
 

 
Rotate them automatically across outreach campaigns and track which version gets the best open and reply rates.
